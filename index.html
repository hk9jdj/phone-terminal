doc: PRIVACY_POLICY platform: Android last_updated: 2026-11-17

TL;DR

We never receive your data. We run no servers, there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and nothing is ever sold. Your commands, notes and settings stay on your device. The optional AI, voice and web-search features send what you ask directly from your phone to the service you use (for example your own AI provider), never to us.

Data on Your Device

TERMINAL AGENT (formerly PHONE TERMINAL) is a terminal-style productivity app. We do not operate any server that receives data from the app, and there are no user accounts.

  • We collect no personal information (name, email, phone number, contacts, location, device identifiers, etc.).
  • Custom commands, notes, settings, the terminal history, websites the AI builds for you and the downloaded on-device AI model are stored only in the app's private storage, which Android keeps unreadable by other apps.
  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of the app's data are disabled. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all of it.
  • Some commands read information on your device only, to show it to you: Android version, device model, uptime, battery, free memory and storage, screen and processor (> sys, > neofetch, > uptime) and your local network address (> ip).
  • To open and connect your apps, the app reads the list of apps that have a launcher icon (names and icons), on your device only (CONNECT APPS, CREATE COMMAND, > open <app>).
  • Clipboard. Read only when you tap PASTE in the ASCII art screen; written only when you ask (copying the art prompt, > pass, or a clipboard command you made).

AI Features (optional)

The AI is off until you turn it on in CONNECT AI. You choose one of two brains:

  • On-device model. Runs entirely on your phone; what you type is not sent anywhere. The model file (about 290 MB) is downloaded once, when you ask for it, from the app's public release page on GitHub. Like any download, GitHub sees your IP address for that request.
  • Your own API key (for example Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter or another compatible service you enter). The app then sends, directly from your phone to that provider: the text you type or say to the AI, the app's instructions, and, only when you use them, the photos or documents you attach with attach and the file names, sizes and text of files in folders you shared with folders add. The provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to that data, and the provider bills you directly. We never see your key or your requests.
  • Your API key is stored on your phone, encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore. You can remove it at any time in CONNECT AI.
  • Folders. The AI agent can see only the folders you pick yourself with Android's folder picker. It never changes, moves or deletes a file unless you answer y to its question first. folders clear removes all access.
  • Websites the AI builds are saved in the app's private storage. When you open one, the page may load fonts or scripts from public web libraries it links to (for example Google Fonts or cdnjs).

Voice Input

  • The microphone is used only while you tap the mic button and speak; it stops by itself when you finish. Android shows its microphone indicator while it is on.
  • Your speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech recognition service (for example the Google app). That service may process the audio on the device or on its maker's servers, under its own privacy policy.
  • The app itself never records, saves or sends audio. It only receives the recognised text, which is then handled exactly like text you typed.

Web Search

  • When you run > search <words>, the words you typed are sent to a search service: with your own AI key, to that AI provider's web search (for example Google Search through Gemini, or Anthropic's web search); without a key, to the free DuckDuckGo Instant Answer API and Wikipedia.
  • No personal data is added to the search. These services see your IP address, as with any web request.
  • > go 1 or > go web opens a result in your browser, where the browser's and website's own policies apply.

File Check (scan)

  • When you pick a file with the shield button or > scan, the app copies it into its private storage, checks it on your phone, and deletes the copy straight after. The file is never uploaded.
  • The check also computes the file's SHA-256 fingerprint. Only if you type > go vt, your browser opens that fingerprint's page on VirusTotal; the file itself is not sent.
  • This is a quick check for common tricks, not a full antivirus.

Permissions

TERMINAL AGENT requests only the permissions below. Each is used only for the feature described.

[1/8]

SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW

Display over other apps

Draws the Floating Terminal HUD, the screen dimmer and the FPS counter on top of the screen.

[2/8]

FOREGROUND_SERVICE + FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE

Keep the HUD running

Keeps the Floating Terminal HUD alive while you use other apps, with an ongoing notification that has a CLOSE HUD button. It runs only after you start the HUD yourself.

[3/8]

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Show notifications

Shows the HUD's ongoing notification (Android 13 and later). You can deny it and the app still works.

[4/8]

INTERNET

Network access

Used only for features you start: > ping, the AI with your own key, downloading the on-device model, > search, and checking Google Play for app updates. No data about you is added.

[5/8]

RECORD_AUDIO

Microphone

Asked the first time you tap the mic button, so you can speak commands (see Voice Input). The app never records or stores audio.

[6/8]

SET_ALARM

Set alarms & timers

Lets > timer start a countdown in your Clock app.

[7/8]

VIBRATE

Vibration

A short tick at the start and end of > matrix.

[8/8]

Files you pick

No storage permission

The app has no permission to read your storage. It sees only the single files or folders you choose yourself in Android's picker (attach, scan, folders add).

Services the App Talks To

  • Zero ads, zero analytics, zero trackers. No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and no data shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • The app contacts other services only for features you use, directly from your phone: the AI provider of your own key, GitHub (on-device model download), DuckDuckGo and Wikipedia (free search), Google Play (purchase and update check), and your phone's speech service (voice input). Each one's own privacy policy applies.
  • Shortcut commands you run yourself (for example > wa, opening a link, or sending a note to an app you connected) open that app or your browser with only the text you typed.
  • Purchases. Handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We never see your payment details; we use Google Play's standard order records only for refunds and support.
  • This web page. In a browser it loads the JetBrains Mono font from Google Fonts. The copy inside the app loads nothing from the internet.

Security

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox, and your AI key is encrypted with the Android Keystore. All network requests use HTTPS. If the app detects that it was modified or tampered with, it deletes the saved AI key and refuses AI calls. We recommend keeping your phone locked and up to date.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children. We collect no personal data from anyone, including children.

Policy Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new "last_updated" date. Changes that affect data handling or permissions are also reflected in the app's Google Play listing.
