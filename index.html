Data Collection & Storage

PHONE TERMINAL is an offline-first productivity tool. We do not operate servers that receive data from the app, and there are no user accounts.

  • We collect no personal information (name, email, phone number, contacts, location, device identifiers, etc.).
  • The app contains zero ads, zero analytics, and zero third-party trackers.
  • We never receive, sell, rent or share your data. Nothing leaves your device except the > ping check described below, and what you choose to pass to another app or your browser.
  • Custom commands, notes and app settings are stored locally only, in the app's private storage, which Android's app sandbox keeps unreadable by other apps.
  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of the app's data are disabled, so this data never leaves your device through backups.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data.
  • Some commands read information on your device only, to show it to you, and never send it anywhere: Android version, device model, uptime, battery level, temperature and charging state, free memory and storage, screen size and processor (> sys, > neofetch, > uptime), and your device's local network address (> ip).
  • To let you open and connect your own apps, the app reads the list of apps on your device that have a launcher icon (their names and icons), on your device only. This is used by CONNECT APPS, CREATE COMMAND and > open <app>. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Clipboard. The app reads your clipboard only when you tap PASTE in the custom ASCII art screen, to paste the art you copied. It writes to your clipboard only when you ask it to: copying the art prompt, > pass (a new password), or a clipboard command you created.

Required Permissions

PHONE TERMINAL requests only the permissions below. Each is used only for the feature described.

[1/5]

SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW

Display over other apps

Required to draw the Floating Terminal HUD, the custom Screen Dimmer filter, and the real-time FPS monitor overlay on top of the screen.

[2/5]

FOREGROUND_SERVICE + FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE

Keep the HUD running

Keep the Floating Terminal HUD alive while you use other apps, with an ongoing notification that has a CLOSE HUD button. It runs only after you start the HUD yourself.

[3/5]

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Show notifications

Show the HUD's ongoing notification (Android 13 and later). No other notifications are sent. You can deny it and the app still works.

[4/5]

INTERNET

Network access

Required strictly for the > ping command, which checks whether the host you type is reachable (your network looks up that name first). If you type no host, and for the home-screen widget's PING shortcut, it checks 8.8.8.8 (Google Public DNS). No data about you is sent.

[5/5]

SET_ALARM

Set alarms & timers

Required by the > timer command to silently schedule background countdown timers with the system Clock app without interrupting the user's active screen.

Ads, Analytics & Trackers

  • Zero ads. The app contains no advertising of any kind.
  • Zero analytics. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • Zero third-party trackers. The app includes no third-party tracking SDKs and shares no data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.
  • The app works offline. The only network requests the app itself makes are the > ping checks described above.
  • Shortcut commands you run yourself (for example web searches, > wa, or sending a note to an app you connected) open your browser or that other app through standard Android intents, passing only the text you typed. From that point, that app's own privacy policy applies.
  • Purchases. The app is bought through Google Play, which handles the payment under Google's own privacy policy. We never see your card or payment details. Google Play gives developers standard order records for paid apps, and we use them only for refunds and support.
  • This web page. When you open this policy in a web browser, the page loads the JetBrains Mono font from Google Fonts. The copy of this policy shown inside the app loads nothing from the internet.

Security

Because your data never leaves your device, its protection relies on Android's app sandbox, which prevents other apps from reading PHONE TERMINAL's private storage. We recommend keeping your device locked and your operating system up to date.

Children's Privacy

Since we collect no personal data from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last_updated" date. Any change that affects data handling or permissions will also be reflected in the app's Google Play listing.
